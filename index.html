02 — About

Every byte tells a story.

I'm a Digital Forensics and Incident Response professional based in Amman. My work sits at the point where an alert stops being a line in a console and becomes a question: what actually happened on this host, how far did it spread, and what do we do in the next hour?

I've spent the last five years inside a national CERT environment — first as a system administrator, then in the SOC, and now in DFIR. That path matters: I've built the systems, monitored them, and taken them apart after they were breached, which shapes how I read evidence and how I write findings for the people who have to act on them.

Day to day that means forensic imaging and triage collection, Windows artefact and registry analysis, memory forensics, log and network review, malware triage, and writing the timeline and report that turns all of it into a decision.

  • Acquisition & triageSound imaging, KAPE collections, chain of custody
  • Host & memory forensicsWindows artefacts, registry, process and injection analysis
  • Hunting & detectionElastic queries, YARA rules, IOC pivoting across the estate
  • ReportingTimelines and findings written for responders and for management
